How to inspect the archive

index.json accounts for every row, including the original digest, disposition,
existing export pointer or new content ID, and transformed-record digest.
metadata.json contains the useful sanitized content. A transformed-record hash
is over canonical UTF-8 JSON: sorted keys, compact separators, ensure_ascii=false,
no trailing newline. MANIFEST.json instead hashes actual exported file bytes.
Private detailed locator and removed-field mappings are kept outside this
publication directory. Nested digests inside a historical record are its
reported references; they are not newly verified runtime inputs.
