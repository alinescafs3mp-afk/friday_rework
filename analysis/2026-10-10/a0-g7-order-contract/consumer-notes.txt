Honest status interpretation
- COMPLETE means complete decoded inventory bytes only. Never native cessation, successful cleanup, release, admission or product PASS.
- A final file may exist while its actual inventory is UNAVAILABLE. None remains None; an actual observed empty list is distinct.
- A refusal with no complete reference, or with referenced bytes unavailable to the caller, returns UNAVAILABLE and STOP_UNCONFIRMED. Reported count/status/digests remain labeled reported_summary, not reconstructed data.
- An AVAILABLE summary plus pinned final bytes must match the decoded full inventory's digest/count/typed bus flags. Missing required refs, wrong version or mixed shapes refuse.
- A late refusal always returns STOP_UNCONFIRMED, even if the earlier snapshot looked normal. Its own exact FD9 close/release diagnostics remain preserved; the decoder cannot infer their truth or perform any transition.
- The full original anchor includes accepted_realtime_ns, which the complete-phase session binding omits. Refusal decoding therefore takes the caller-pinned full original_anchor explicitly and also crosschecks its monotonic/boottime clocks. It does not reconstruct a lossy two-clock anchor.
- observation_refs/original_attempt_ref/dropins are retained as opaque diagnostics. This small decoder does not resolve preflight or cleanup witnesses, and makes no completeness/authority claim about them.

Validation and limitations
Actual18318710-byte phase with545transports is passed through AST-selected accepted producer persistence into a fixed-size V2 final envelope and recovered exactly by the decoder. The same selected producer emits late refusal, then the decoder preserves the STOP outcome. Only pure/helper file-fixture code is selected; whole producer/entry are never imported/executed. No qualification, model, native/socket/service/worker/reload/lock/grant/new generation or retry is run. Original producer, original manifest/entry, actual phase and closed readonly sources remain unchanged. One-save, cleanup and FD9 semantics are unchanged producer code; fixtures check no repeated cleanup/save or FD9 release. Independent source review and final assembled source binding remain required. Existing owner authorization is not revoked and no extra owner permission is invented; the actual interactive terminal/sudo requirement remains technical and is not automated.
